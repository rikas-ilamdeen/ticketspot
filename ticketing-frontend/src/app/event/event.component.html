Route Highlights & Terrain.

The 30 km course navigates through the dramatic high-elevation topography of the Central Highlands, blending historical railway segments with remote wilderness trails.

7 km Rail Run

Following high mountain railway corridors with dramatic valley views.

15 km Forest Trail

Winding deep into native cloud forests, wetlands, and ridge paths.

Udaweriya Peak

Challenging ascents reaching panoramic highland tea estate peaks.

Horton Plains Road

Traversing rugged high plateau stretches with mist and brisk winds.

Devil's Staircase

The iconic final descent featuring dramatic hairpin curves and stone roads.

Stream Crossings & Terrain

Natural water crossings, rocky paths, steep climbs, and mountain descents.
